# Unplugged Grant Scout

This repo holds the grant scout for Unplugged Wellness, a Salt Lake City 501(c)(3) (Bike Club and SLC community events). Owner: Liam McGee, board member and co-writer. Liam is not a developer; explain GitHub steps in plain language.

## Layout
- `skill/unplugged-grant-scout/SKILL.md`: the scout's instructions. Source of truth. After editing, zip the folder and re-upload it on claude.ai (Customize > Skills).
- `scheduled-task/monthly-prompt.md`: the monthly scheduled task's settings and prompt. Keep it in sync with the skill.
- `tracker/`: CSV snapshots of the tracker. The live tracker is the Google Sheet, not these files.
